P Vault for Android

Android 8.0 or newer. No account needed. Your first backup goes to the Google Drive folder you choose.

Download APK

The file opens from GitHub, the same place the app checks for new versions.

Installing

A two-minute update.

Android asks you to confirm the install because the APK comes from outside the Play Store. That is expected.

  1. 1

    Download the APK

    Tap the download button. The newest P Vault build is fetched from GitHub Releases.

  2. 2

    Open the file

    If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps for this one install.

  3. 3

    Update over the old version

    Tap Update. Do not uninstall first. Your vault, chats, settings and Drive folder connection stay in place.

Your data stays put. P Vault stores everything on your device and copies backups to the Google Drive folder you picked. Updating the app does not erase either one.
