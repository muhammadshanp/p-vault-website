How to update

  1. 1

    Download the APK

    Tap Download update. Your browser saves P-Vault.apk to Downloads.

  2. 2

    Open it and tap Update

    If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Update. Do not uninstall the old app first.

  3. 3

    Open P Vault

    Your data, reel text, images and PDFs are right where you left them.

Before you start, check that your last backup to Google Drive has finished.
